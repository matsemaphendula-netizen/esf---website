Welcome to ESF

We are a youth Bible study fellowship dedicated to empowering students in Eswatini with the Word of God.

About Us

ESF is a Christian fellowship based in tertiary institutions across Eswatini. We aim to nurture spiritual growth, leadership, and community among students.

Vision: A generation of students anchored in Scripture, thriving in community, and impacting society for Christ.

Mission: To equip students through Bible study, prayer, mentorship, and outreach initiatives.

Our Programs

Weekly Bible studies, prayer meetings, outreach events, and leadership training for students.
